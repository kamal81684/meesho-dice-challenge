pricing.py · floor_price()

The 0%-commission floor P₀

Every risk cost is priced in before the first parcel ships. Below P₀ the seller misses their target, and below break-even every order loses money.

P₀ = COGS + Labour + Packaging + Fees + rrto·Crto + rret·Cdamage + Target 1 − (GST + TCS/TDS)

lifecycle.py · bandit.py · POST /api/lifecycle

Lifecycle engine

A state machine reads sales signals and picks the stage. In the Scale stage a LinUCB bandit nudges price, and guardrails remove any move outside P₀ to Pmax.

DAYS 1–21

Launch

Zero reviews. Launch wedge at P₀ + 3% to seed the first 25 orders.

target margin 3–5%
DAYS 22–90

Scale

Rating and conversion hold. The bandit nudges price by +₹10 or +₹25 each cycle.

target margin 15–20%
DAYS 91–180

Defense

A rival undercuts by 6% or more, or conversion drops 15%. Hold price and offer 2- and 3-packs.

target margin 10–12%
DAY 181+ OR STALE

Salvage

Fewer than 2 sales a week and stock older than 120 days. Drop below P₀ to free up cash.

target margin −5% to 0%

scripts/backtest.py · Slide 8 scenario

Surat cotton suit backtest

Same simulated market for both sellers, 150 days, averaged over 20 random seeds. Manual seller: ₹349 by offline markup, no fabric card, raises to ₹399 on day 120. Vyapar-AI: launches at ₹379 (P₀ = ₹367), adds the fabric card on Day 0, and the bandit climbs inside the band.

These results come from a synthetic market model. They show the mechanism works; they are not a forecast of real Meesho impact.
